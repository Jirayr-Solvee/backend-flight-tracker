# Aircraft-story notifications

The worker is disabled by default. Production enablement requires `SOFLY_STORY_PUSH_ENABLED=1` and an exact allowlist such as `SOFLY_STORY_PUSH_RELEASES=3.9.3:142,3.9.4:143` in `/etc/sofly/story-push.env`. The old single version/build fields remain a fallback. Only verified supported releases belong in the allowlist. Unknown builds, Debug, TestFlight, disabled permissions/preferences, expired subscriptions and registrations older than 30 days are excluded. Existing flight status alerts are unchanged.

The app refreshes its device capability/version, language, time zone and permission/preference on launch/foreground and preference changes. A legacy registration clears capability. Settings allow turning story pushes off independently of flight alerts. Device metadata is removed with account deletion.

Target two or three shared stories/day when suitable stories exist, not a guarantee. The worker checks every ten minutes and sends in local windows 10:00–11:59, 15:00–16:59, 19:00–20:59. No catch-up outside those windows. Three-hour minimum spacing and maximum three shared campaigns in any rolling 24 hours apply per subscriber; every eligible device on that account receives the same campaign. One story is frozen per local day and slot, so subscribers receive the same selection in their own daytime window. Cached stories must be from a recently refreshed feed, at most 72 hours old when selected, and have an editorial score of at least 65. Existing safe-publication and translation review rules remain mandatory. No extra AI calls or filler. Repeated normalized transmissions are suppressed across campaigns for 90 days.

A published story explicitly matching a subscriber's saved flight can generate an additional alert within two hours of the transmission. Attribution requires the aircraft registration, flight number and flight time window to match; a general airport bulletin never qualifies. Flight-specific story alerts do not consume shared campaign slots. A subscriber does not receive the same story twice on one device, or as a flight extra after the shared campaign. Existing AeroDataBox flight status/gate/time alerts remain independent.

SQLite `BEGIN IMMEDIATE` freezes campaigns and reserves each device send before APNs, across independent workers. A failed, timed-out or interrupted attempt consumes its reservation; uncertain sends are not retried by our scheduler. This is not a guarantee of APNs delivery or device rendering. `accepted` means APNs returned 200. The APNs notification ID correlates with the existing encrypted notification-open analytics. The story-detail `experience_action` records the message ID and successful presentation separately.

Use the installed ten-minute timer only after an exact-build TestFlight test of cold/background taps and language/preference behavior. Never switch the normal worker to TestFlight or development. Single-device release QA is separate, explicitly scoped, and must not broadcast.

An opted-in tester can receive the same scheduled story alerts without a paid App Store entitlement through the separate `sofly-story-push-qa.timer`. Its root-owned `/etc/sofly/story-push-qa.env` must set `SOFLY_STORY_PUSH_ENABLED=1`, `SOFLY_STORY_PUSH_QA_ENABLED=1`, `SOFLY_STORY_PUSH_ENVIRONMENT=testflight`, one exact `SOFLY_STORY_PUSH_QA_DEVICE_ID` UUID, and an exact `SOFLY_STORY_PUSH_RELEASES` pair. The QA worker queries only that device, checks current ownership, APNs token, TestFlight environment, supported build, permission/preference, and registration freshness both before reservation and before send. Only that device may skip the paid-entitlement check; production never can. The same frozen campaigns, flight matching, reservations, three-per-day cap, spacing and editorial filters apply. This is notification QA access, not a StoreKit entitlement or premium feature grant. Stop the QA timer and remove its environment file when the tester opts out or changes devices. No device ID or APNs token belongs in Git.

`GET /cockpit/stories/{id}` requires authentication and reads only retained stories (seven days). The push contains an opaque story ID, no invented flight ID. If a message has expired or the service is unavailable, the app shows a dismissible localized retry screen.

Verification: `python -m unittest tests.test_story_push tests.test_story_push_registration tests.test_cockpit_api tests.test_cockpit_ingestion`. App build/device tests are separate. Retained delivery identifiers are purged after 90 days on active worker runs and removed during account deletion; no notification text or token is logged by this worker.
